#1109: validator staking and slashing.

ValidatorStaking is stored on the contract ([crate::PropertyBridge::staking]) and tracks the credit-based stake of each registered validator. Approvals for bridge requests require a staked-weight quorum (STAKED_QUORUM_BPS of total_staked), not a bare signature count; a validator that flip-flops its vote on a request is slashed SLASH_PERCENT of its current stake into the slash pool.

The stake is a nominal economic bond held in the ledger (no token transfers): validators self-stake in PropertyBridge::stake_validator, withdraw via withdraw_stake, and are penalised via slash_validator or automatically on a conflicting vote.
