Expand description

Largest init_params payload accepted by deploy_contract, in bytes.

init_params is the SCALE-encoded constructor argument list that gets forwarded to the target contract. Nothing about it is bounded on the way in, so an oversized payload is copied into the transaction, re-encoded for the cross-contract call, and then written into the deployment record’s footprint — all before anything rejects it. 4 KiB is far above any realistic ink! constructor signature (the largest templates in templates.rs encode to tens of bytes) while still bounding the work a single call can force.
